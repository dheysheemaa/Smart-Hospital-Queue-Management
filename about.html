🏥 About MediCare+

Making healthcare smarter.

MediCare+ is a smart hospital queue management system designed to simplify patient registration, queue tracking and waiting-time management.

WHO WE ARE

A simpler way to manage hospital queues.

Traditional hospital queues can result in long waiting times, crowded reception areas and difficulty tracking patient turns.

MediCare+ provides a digital approach to queue management. Patients can register their details, receive a digital token and monitor the live queue from a single platform.

The system also helps hospital staff monitor patient status, departments, waiting time and queue progress more efficiently.
